# Source Evaluation Method

Evaluate each source across:
1. Authority and issuing body.
2. Primary vs secondary nature.
3. Publication/release date and version.
4. Methodological transparency.
5. Reproducibility and inspectability.
6. Scope fit to the claim.
7. Independence and conflicts of interest.
8. License and access restrictions.
9. Maintenance status for software repositories.
10. Known limitations and conflicting sources.

Record a qualitative assessment and explain it. Do not reduce source quality to one unexplained score.
