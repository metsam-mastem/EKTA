## Validation checks
- Every activity has at least one R.
- Each activity has one clearly identified A unless an explicitly approved governance model says otherwise.
- C and I are used only where they add value.
- Roles, not only personal names, are identified where possible.
- Decision authority is not inferred from RACI; link the decision-rights or delegation matrix.
- Resolve overload, gaps, duplicate accountability, and conflicts.
- Record approval and review after material scope or team changes.

RACI is a responsibility-assignment aid, not a substitute for job descriptions, contracts, decision policy, or workflow design.
