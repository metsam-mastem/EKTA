This checklist is an issue-spotting tool, not legal, tax, investment, or regulatory advice.

## 1. Strategic fit
- [ ] Shared objectives and measurable value are explicit.
- [ ] Scope, exclusions, assumptions, and duration are clear.
- [ ] Each party's incentives and success criteria are understood.

## 2. Counterparty and authority
- [ ] Legal identity and registration checked through appropriate sources.
- [ ] Signatory authority verified.
- [ ] Relevant references, track record, and conflicts reviewed.

## 3. Contributions and economics
- [ ] Cash, labor, assets, data, facilities, and other contributions valued.
- [ ] Cost allocation, revenue share, payment terms, tax treatment, and audit rights reviewed.
- [ ] Budget, funding dependencies, and financial exposure documented.

## 4. Governance and operations
- [ ] Steering and operational roles defined.
- [ ] Decision rights, reserved matters, approvals, and escalation documented.
- [ ] Deliverables, milestones, acceptance, change control, and reporting defined.

## 5. Legal, IP, data, and confidentiality
- [ ] NDA / confidentiality requirements reviewed.
- [ ] Background and foreground IP ownership and licenses defined.
- [ ] Data controller/processor roles and lawful data handling assessed where relevant.
- [ ] Privacy, security, export, sector, employment, and competition-law issues assessed where relevant.
- [ ] Insurance, liability, indemnity, warranties, and limitations reviewed by qualified counsel.

## 6. Risk and resilience
- [ ] Delivery, financial, reputational, cyber, operational, and dependency risks assessed.
- [ ] Business continuity and incident responsibilities assigned.
- [ ] Third-party and subcontractor dependencies identified.

## 7. Exit and dispute
- [ ] Term, renewal, termination triggers, notice, and cure periods reviewed.
- [ ] Dispute resolution and governing law reviewed.
- [ ] Handover, data return/deletion, IP continuity, and access revocation defined.
