## 3. Decision and responsibility methods

- **RACI / RASCI:** responsibility assignment matrix. It is not a complete decision framework.
- **DACI:** decision driver, approver, contributors, informed.
- **RAPID:** recommend, agree, perform, input, decide.
- **Delegation of Authority:** organization-specific approval and spending limits; requires formal approval and version control.
- **ADR:** decision record for architecture or consequential technical choices; preserve context, options, decision, consequences, and status.

Use framework-owner materials when documenting exact definitions, licensing, and required practices.
